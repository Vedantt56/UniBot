DEMO STATE UNIVERSITY — FICTIONAL SAMPLE DOCUMENT
Attendance Policy for Academic Year 2026–27
Section 1 — Minimum attendance
For this fictional demonstration university, students must maintain at least 75 percent attendance in each registered course to be eligible for the regular end-semester examination.

Section 2 — Attendance exceptions
Students requesting an exemption must submit a written application to the Academic Office. Approval is not guaranteed and must be issued in writing by the Examination Committee. This policy does not state a minimum attendance for exemption eligibility.

Section 3 — Calculation
Attendance is calculated for each course separately from teaching sessions conducted and sessions attended. The final attendance record is published by the academic department.
