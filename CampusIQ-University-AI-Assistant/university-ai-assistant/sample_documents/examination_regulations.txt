Examination Regulations for 2026–27
Section 1 — Grading
End-semester examinations and continuous assessment are combined according to the course evaluation plan. No universal percentage weightage applies across all courses.

Section 2 — Backlog examination
Students with a failed course may register for a supplementary examination during the dates announced by the Controller of Examinations. Registration requires submission of the published form.

Section 3 — Results
Results are released on the university student portal. The chatbot has no access to individual students' marks or results.
